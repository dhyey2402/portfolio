01 — About

Engineer by training.
Builder by default.

I'm a Computer Science undergrad at Karnavati University's UnitedWorld Institute of Technology, based in Ahmedabad. I care about the unglamorous parts of software — clean data models, sensible APIs, systems that stay correct under load — and the polished parts people actually feel: speed, clarity, and interfaces that get out of the way.

Most of what I know came from shipping. Over the last two years I've built ERPs, SaaS platforms, ML pipelines and developer tools, usually end-to-end: schema to API to interface. If there's a hard problem with real users on the other side, I want in.
